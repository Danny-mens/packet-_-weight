Packet weight calculator

Scan a photo of the sheet or type the readings. Averages, max, min and range update as you go.

Scan a sheet

Lay the sheet flat, keep it straight and well lit, and fit the whole page in the photo. Cells outlined in amber were hard to read, so check them against the sheet.

Scan settings (API key)

Needed only when you open this app outside Claude, such as from your Home Screen. The key is stored on this phone only and is sent only to Anthropic.

Paste readings instead

Paste rows like "5:30 PM, M/C-3, 27.24, 28.74, 28.27, 28.50, 28.24, 28.19" (time, machine, five readings, then the written average if you have it). Data in the app's own format works too.

Machines

With a target and tolerance set, readings outside the limits turn red. If you also type the average written on the sheet, any block where it differs from the calculated one is flagged.

Results for the whole shift

Total AVG is the mean of every reading entered for that machine. Range is max minus min. "All" pools every reading from every machine. "Avg of machines" is the average of each row across the machines that have readings (for example, the mean of the machines' Total AVG values, and the mean of their MAX WT values).
